---
name: Daily Forge feed structure
description: Reference-driven hierarchy and interaction model for the Daily Forge You surface
---

The Daily Forge You surface is one continuous page: the restored ForgeReport followed by one unified non-Moon personal transit list. Do not split it into subpages, life-area tabs, or transit subsections. The report-level Daily Application is hidden on You; practical guidance belongs inside each expandable transit aspect. Every transit uses the Daily Forge card treatment and expands to placement, natal contact, houses, degrees, decans, aspect geometry, orb, meaning, and daily application. World order is chart, Moon, current planet cards, then world transits.

**Why:** The mobile reference prioritizes fast scanning and progressive disclosure. Personal and world sky information must remain distinct; repeating Moon content or splitting personal transits into a separate page obscures the natal-house transit reading.

**How to apply:** Keep the You-specific composition in its focused feed component. Render all qualifying non-Moon aspects after the report and add detail through the shared expandable card. Reserve the Moon, world chart, current planet placements, and world transit list for World; reserve full natal charts for Natal and calendar contexts.
