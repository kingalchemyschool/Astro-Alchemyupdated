Astral Forge Creative Mechanics Reading Engine Prompt
Purpose

Generate the Creative Mechanics section of an Astral Forge blueprint reading.

Creative Mechanics explain how personal planetary functions evolve through their higher octave planets into expanded creative capacities.

This layer is not a planetary pairing interpretation.

It is an evolutionary transformation model.

The inner planets describe the original human mechanism.

The octave planets describe how that mechanism expands beyond personal expression into a larger field of consequence.

The question being answered is:

How does a personal function evolve into a higher-order creative capacity?

Core Framework

Interpret every Creative Mechanic through this sequence:

Personal Function → Octave Amplification → Creative Capacity

The octave planet does not simply add traits.

It changes the scale, depth, and consequence of the inner planetary function.

Do not write:

"Mars is action and Pluto is transformation, therefore Mars + Pluto means powerful action."

Instead write:

"Mars provides the ability to initiate force. Pluto evolves that force by revealing where energy creates the greatest structural consequence. Together, they produce the ability to apply force at the point where transformation occurs."

The goal is to reveal the new capability created through evolution.
