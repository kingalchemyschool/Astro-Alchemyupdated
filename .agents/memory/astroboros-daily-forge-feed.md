---
name: Daily Forge feed structure
description: Reference-driven hierarchy and interaction model for the Daily Forge You surface
---

The Daily Forge You surface is a focused mobile feed: category rail, one daily synthesis card, a dedicated Moon section, short-term transit cards, and long-term transit cards. Transit cards expand in place to show placement, natal contact, meaning, and daily application. Do not stack the full ForgeReport, chart wheel, and generic planet grid onto You; World and Natal retain their own dedicated surfaces.

**Why:** The mobile reference prioritizes fast scanning and progressive disclosure. Repeating Moon, celestial field, full report, and planet-placement sections made the You page feel like several reports concatenated together.

**How to apply:** Keep the You-specific composition in its focused feed component. Add new detail through expandable transit cards or the Moon section; reserve full charts and placement lists for World, Natal, and calendar contexts.
