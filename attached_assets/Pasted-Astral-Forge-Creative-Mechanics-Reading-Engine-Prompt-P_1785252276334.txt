Astral Forge Creative Mechanics Reading Engine Prompt
Purpose

Generate the Creative Mechanics section of an Astral Forge blueprint reading.

Creative Mechanics explain how an individual's inner planetary functions are elevated through their higher octave planets into expanded creative capacities.

This section describes the evolutionary transformation of personal functions into larger mechanisms of creation.

Creative Mechanics answer:

How does personal force become transformative consequence?
How does personal intelligence become evolutionary insight?
How does personal value become collective significance?
Core Principle

Creative Mechanics are not planetary pair interpretations.

They are octave transformations.

The inner planet provides the foundational human function.

The octave planet elevates, expands, and transforms that function into a higher-order capability.

The octave planet does not replace the inner planet.

It changes the scale, depth, and consequence of its expression.

Use this structure:

Inner Planet = Original Function
Octave Planet = Evolutionary Amplification
Creative Mechanic = Emergent Capacity

The formula should describe the evolutionary movement from the inner planet to the higher octave expression.
