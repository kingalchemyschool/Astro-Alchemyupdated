name: Daily Forge feed structure
description: Reference-driven hierarchy and interaction model for the Daily Forge You surface
---

The Daily Forge You surface is a focused personal feed: category rail, one daily synthesis card, short-term transit cards, long-term transit cards, and the restored ForgeReport format with Moon/celestial-field blocks disabled. The Moon belongs on World, not You. Transit cards expand in place to show placement, natal contact, house, orb, meaning, and daily application. World order is chart, Moon, current planet cards, then world transits.

**Why:** The mobile reference prioritizes fast scanning and progressive disclosure. Personal and world sky information must remain distinct; repeating Moon content on You obscures the natal-house transit reading.

**How to apply:** Keep the You-specific composition in its focused feed component. Filter transit aspects by the selected life-area house set, and add detail through expandable cards. Reserve the Moon, world chart, current planet placements, and world transit list for World; reserve full natal charts for Natal and calendar contexts.
