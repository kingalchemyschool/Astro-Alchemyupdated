Rewrite the Blueprint Reading using the following writing rules:

The planetary functions (Vision, Reception, Impact, Communication, Expansion, Value, Foundation, etc.) are part of the internal architecture of the system, but they are not the language the reader should repeatedly encounter. The function names should orient the section, not dominate the interpretation.

For each planet:

1. Use the planet + function as the section title:
Example:
☽ Moon — Reception

2. In the first paragraph, explain the function naturally in 1–2 sentences:
Do not define it like a textbook. Explain what role it plays in the creative process and how energy moves through the system.

Example:
"Receiving the vision established by the Sun, the Moon converts that original pattern into lived experience. It governs what registers as true, what the body recognizes before the mind explains it, and how the external world is emotionally processed."

3. After the opening explanation, stop repeatedly naming the function.

Do NOT write:
- "Perception does this..."
- "Reception allows..."
- "Impact creates..."
- "Communication translates..."

Instead, describe the actual human experience:
- what the person notices
- how they process information
- how they make decisions
- how they create change
- where their energy flows naturally
- where their challenges appear
- what the placement produces in real life

The reader should never have to understand the internal terminology to understand the interpretation.

---

For aspects:

Do not explain aspects as relationships between abstract functions.

Avoid:
"The 2.1° trine to Saturn allows Perception to draw naturally on consolidation."

This forces the reader to decode the system.

Instead write:
"Your connection to Saturn gives your emotional world structure. Rather than reacting to every experience immediately, you have the ability to organize what you feel, extract lessons from it, and build lasting stability from what you learn."

Every aspect interpretation must answer:

- What does this planetary relationship create?
- How does it affect the person's behavior?
- What ability does it strengthen?
- What challenge does it create?
- How does it influence the person's life path?

---

The final reading should feel like a high-level psychological and strategic blueprint, not an explanation of the astrology framework.

The reader should walk away understanding:
- how their energy operates
- how they create
- how they make decisions
- where they encounter friction
- how they convert potential into external consequence

Do not overuse proprietary terminology. Translate the system into clear, precise human language.

Write with depth, sophistication, and clarity. Assume the reader is intelligent but unfamiliar with the framework.
