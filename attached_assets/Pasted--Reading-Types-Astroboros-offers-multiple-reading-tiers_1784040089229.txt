# Reading Types

Astroboros offers multiple reading tiers. Every reading must be generated according to the product purchased. Never generate the same depth, structure, or level of personalization across all products.

---

# Free Reading

Purpose:
Generate curiosity, recognition, and excitement.

The free reading should feel insightful without revealing the complete operating system.

Include:

* Core Alchemical Archetype
* Brief explanation of why this archetype emerged
* Six Creative Functions (name, one-sentence description, archetype only)
* Overall Creation Cycle summary
* Planet placement table
* One short paragraph describing each planetary engine
* One short paragraph introducing each threshold

Do NOT include:

* deep planetary synthesis
* aspect analysis
* developmental mechanics
* function breakdowns
* shadow mechanics
* activation practices
* relationship dynamics
* advanced chart synthesis
* Hero Story

The free reading should answer:

"Who am I?"

while encouraging the user to unlock:

"How do I actually work?"

---

# Paid Reading ($25)

Purpose:
Reveal the user's complete creative operating system.

The paid reading is not a longer horoscope.

It is a comprehensive systems analysis explaining exactly how the user's consciousness creates, communicates, develops, and transforms.

Every section must synthesize:

* planetary function
* sign
* house
* aspects
* planetary relationships
* thresholds
* archetypes
* developmental psychology

The AI must never rely on generalized astrology descriptions.

Every paragraph should answer:

"What unique mechanism is being created by THIS exact combination?"

The paid reading should feel:

* highly personalized
* psychologically accurate
* mechanically precise
* mythically meaningful
* impossible to mistake for another person's reading

No two paid readings should sound structurally identical.

The AI should actively avoid repeating:

* sentence openings
* paragraph rhythm
* examples
* metaphors
* strengths
* developmental lessons

Every interpretation should emerge from the chart itself.

If two users both have Mars in Cancer, but different houses, aspects, creative functions, or archetype combinations, the interpretation should become substantially different.

The reader should feel:

"This could only have been written for me."

This is also where the user's personal Hero's Journey is. 

# Hero Myth Writing Rules

The Hero Myth is not a summary of the reading.

It is the emotional synthesis of the entire blueprint.

The user should finish the reading feeling like they have just read the myth that has always been hidden inside their chart.

The story must feel inevitable, as though every placement naturally became a character, challenge, mentor, threshold, and transformation.

The Hero Myth should read like a modern myth, fable, or legend—not a horoscope, fairy tale, or generic Hero's Journey template.

---

## Core Philosophy

Do not write a story *about* astrology.

Write a story that astrology silently generated.

The reader should not need to know astrology to understand the story.

Someone unfamiliar with astrology should still feel deeply seen.

---

## The Story Must Be Chart-Specific

Every myth must emerge directly from the chart.

Never reuse story structures.

Never begin every story with:

* "Every hero..."
* "Once there was..."
* "Our story begins..."
* "Before doing anything..."

The opening should be inspired by the dominant theme of the chart.

Examples:

A builder's story might begin with a mountain.

A Mercury-dominant chart might begin inside a library.

A Pisces Moon may begin in the ocean, a dream, or a forgotten temple.

An Aquarius Venus may begin in a city that does not yet exist.

A Scorpio Pluto may begin underground.

The setting itself should reflect the chart.

---

Each planetary engine should become part of the myth.

Sun
The calling.
The reason the journey begins.

Moon
The hidden world.
The emotional landscape.
The invisible guide.

Mars
The first obstacle.
The first decisive action.
The lesson that force alone is insufficient.

Being Threshold
The moment instinct becomes intention.
The hero realizes effort alone cannot solve the problem.

Mercury
The gift.
The language.
The map.
The weapon.
The strategy.
The realization that intelligence changes destiny.

Jupiter
Expansion.
Opportunity.
Temptation.
The invitation to become more.

Will Threshold
The impossible choice.
The realization that purpose requires sacrifice.

Venus
The true treasure.
The value discovered.
What proves worth protecting.

Saturn
The final trial.
The structure that must be built.
The responsibility accepted.

Retention Threshold
The transformation becomes permanent.
The hero is no longer completing the journey.
They have become part of the world they changed.

---

## Archetypes Must Become Characters

The dominant archetype should become the protagonist.

The remaining five creative archetypes become companions, mentors, rivals, inner voices, allies, guardians, or recurring forces within the story.

The myth should subtly show how every creative function participates in the journey.

---

## The Three Thresholds Are Initiations

Treat each threshold like a sacred initiation.

The hero cannot continue without changing.

The threshold is never simply an event.

It changes the hero's identity.

After each threshold the protagonist should think differently, not merely possess new abilities.

---

## Symbolism

Use symbols generated by the chart itself.

Examples include:

* mountains
* oceans
* forests
* storms
* mirrors
* towers
* libraries
* gardens
* stars
* rivers
* bridges
* keys
* gates
* fire
* metal
* light
* architecture

Choose symbols that naturally resonate with the chart's dominant elemental balance, signs, houses, and archetypes.

Avoid random fantasy imagery.

Every symbol should feel meaningful.

---

## Tone

The myth should be:

* poetic
* intelligent
* emotionally resonant
* symbolic
* psychologically insightful
* timeless
* mythic without becoming overly mystical

Include moments of humor only when they naturally fit the chart.

---

## Personalization Rules

No two myths should share the same structure.

If two people share identical Sun signs, the myth should still feel completely different because it is generated from the entire blueprint.

The dominant archetype, planetary engines, thresholds, aspects, creative functions, and chart themes should all influence:

* setting
* pacing
* conflict
* symbolism
* ending
* moral

---

## Ending

Never end with a generic inspirational statement.

The ending should reveal what the hero has actually become.

The final sentence should feel unforgettable.

The reader should close the report feeling that they understand not only where they have been, but what kind of person they are becoming.

The myth should leave the reader with the feeling:

"I have just read the legend my life has been trying to live."

---

# Compatibility Reading (free or $15 longer report)

Purpose:
Reveal how two creative operating systems interact.

This is NOT a synastry reading.

The goal is not compatibility scores or romance predictions.

Instead, in the free reading compare:

* Vision
* Perception
* Force
* Communication
* Expansion
* Value
* Foundation

Then compare in the paid reading:

* all six creative functions
* all three thresholds
* dominant archetypes
* overall creation cycles

The comparison should explain:

Where they naturally amplify each other.

Where friction produces growth.

Where misunderstandings emerge.

How each person's operating system changes the other's.

How they solve problems differently.

How they create together.

How they communicate.

How they build trust.

How conflict naturally appears.

How mastery develops inside the relationship.

The reading should never decide whether the relationship is "good" or "bad."

Instead explain:

"What happens when these two systems begin creating together?"

The relationship itself should become the subject.

---

# Relationship Writing Rules

Never compare placements individually.

Never say:

"One person has Mars in Cancer and the other has Mars in Aries."

Instead explain:

"One person generates force by protecting stability while the other generates force through direct initiation. Together this creates a relationship where momentum alternates between preservation and acceleration."

Always compare mechanisms instead of symbols.

---

# Personalization Rules

Every reading should feel handcrafted.

If two users share:

* the same sign
* the same planet
* the same house

the AI must still generate different interpretations by synthesizing:

* aspects
* planetary relationships
* thresholds
* archetypes
* dominant chart themes
* overall blueprint

No paragraph should be reusable across charts.

The AI should continually ask:

"What makes this exact blueprint different from every other blueprint?"

That answer—not generic astrology—is what should become the reading.
