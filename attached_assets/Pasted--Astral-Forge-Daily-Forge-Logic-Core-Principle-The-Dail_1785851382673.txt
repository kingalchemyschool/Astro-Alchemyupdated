# Astral Forge Daily Forge Logic

## Core Principle

The Daily Forge is a synthesis engine, not a transit list.

The system should analyze the entire celestial field first, determine the dominant developmental process, and then generate the report.

Individual transits provide evidence for the interpretation. They are not the interpretation itself.

---

# Internal Calculation Layer

Before generating text, calculate:

## 1. Dominant Arena

Rank houses by:

* Number of significant transit activations
* Exactness of aspects
* Planetary importance
* Repetition of themes
* Presence of conjunctions/oppositions
* Natal sensitivity

Select the highest-weighted house.

Do not display activation counts.

---

## 2. Dominant Planetary Pattern

Identify repeated planetary themes.

Example:

Moon + Saturn + Mercury

Possible synthesis:
emotion + structure + mental refinement

Do not interpret planets individually first.

Find the shared developmental process.

---

## 3. Developmental Process

Convert the planetary pattern into one capacity being refined.

Examples:

Saturn emphasis:
discipline, durability, responsibility

Mars emphasis:
direction, execution, initiative

Mercury emphasis:
translation, understanding, decision-making

Venus emphasis:
value, attraction, cultivation

Jupiter emphasis:
growth, expansion, capacity

Moon emphasis:
processing, responsiveness, integration

Sun emphasis:
identity, purpose, expression

---

# Reader-Facing Structure

## 1. Dominant Arena

Must appear before aspects.

Format:

House Name · Archetypal Function

2–3 sentence synthesis.

Rules:

* Explain why this area dominates.
* Do not mention activation numbers.
* Do not repeat house meaning under every aspect.

---

# 2. Celestial Field

List significant aspects.

Each aspect requires:

Planetary Contact

Short Archetypal Title

One or two sentence interpretation.

Format:

Moon Conjunct Natal Saturn

**Emotion meets structure.**

Interpretation.

Rules:

* Avoid "Moon brings..."
* Avoid dictionary definitions.
* Explain the interaction between forces.
* Avoid repeating house meanings.

---

# 3. Today's Theme

Generate one sentence.

Requirements:

* 8–15 words
* Describes the entire day
* No astrology terminology
* Memorable and practical

Example:

Build lasting capacity through focused commitment.

---

# 4. The Forge

This replaces "Celestial State."

Purpose:

Explain how the entire chart works together.

Answer:

"What is the central developmental tension today?"

Rules:

* 150–200 words
* Reference only dominant influences
* No transit-by-transit summary
* No repeated definitions

---

# 5. What Is Being Refined

Purpose:

Translate astrology into alchemical development.

Rules:

* One metaphor only.
* Connect to the dominant capacity.
* Avoid excessive symbolism.
* End with a practical insight.

Length:

150–250 words.

---

# 6. Forge Principle

One sentence.

Requirements:

* Under 10 words preferred.
* Universal principle.
* Not motivational language.

Examples:

Structure creates lasting capacity.

Attention becomes ability through repetition.

---

# 7. Journal Prompt

One question.

Must connect:

Dominant House + Developmental Process

Avoid generic reflection questions.

---

# 8. Daily Application

Three sections:

## Do

Concrete action.

## Avoid

Behavior that disperses the day's potential.

## Practice

Repeatable behavior that develops the capacity.

Each under 40 words.

---

# 9. Closing Reflection

Purpose:

Leave the user with the enduring lesson.

Rules:

* Do not summarize transits.
* Do not repeat the theme.
* Do not explain astrology.
* End with the capacity being carried forward.

---

# Style Rules

Avoid:

* "The universe wants..."
* "You are being asked..."
* "This energy means..."
* Excessive mystical language
* Transit dictionary explanations
* Repeating the same idea in multiple sections

Prefer:

* "The field emphasizes..."
* "The pattern favors..."
* "The tension reveals..."
* "The process develops..."

The final report should feel like an alchemical practice journal, not an astrology forecast.
