The synastry matrix must classify contacts from exact sidereal longitude separation, not from sign-to-sign distance. Sign relationships may add context, but they must never override the calculated aspect or orb.

**Why:** The reference comparison includes Partner A's Aries Sun 1°20′ and Partner B's Sagittarius Sun 26°29′ as a square at approximately 4°50′. A sign-distance shortcut incorrectly rendered this as a trine.

**How to apply:** Keep the functional Lab cards and the complete matrix grounded in the same exact cross-chart calculation. The full matrix includes all ten planetary points, both Ascendants, and quincunxes in the synastry model only; do not broaden unrelated natal/transit aspect types just to support the comparison.
