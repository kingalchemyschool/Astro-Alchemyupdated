# Daily Forge Generation Rules

The Daily Forge is not a list of transit interpretations. It is a synthesis of the day's celestial field into one coherent narrative. Every section should be generated from the chart as a whole rather than from individual aspects.

## Overall Structure

1. Today's Theme
2. Celestial Field
3. Today's Energy
4. What Is Being Refined
5. Forge Principle
6. Journal Prompt
7. Daily Application
8. Closing Reflection

---

# Generation Order

Before writing any text, calculate the following:

## Step 1 — Rank the dominant influences

Determine the day's hierarchy using objective criteria.

Rank by:

* Number of transits affecting the same natal house.
* Number of transits affecting the same natal planet.
* Exactness of aspect (orb).
* Aspect type (conjunctions generally outweigh sextiles, etc.).
* Repetition of the same planetary archetype.
* Repetition of the same life arena (house).
* Whether a transit is selected as Primary.

This hierarchy determines the report.

Do not simply describe every transit equally.

---

## Step 2 — Identify the dominant house

If multiple significant transits occur within the same natal house, generate a single synthesis describing that house.

Do not repeat the house description inside every transit.

Instead display:

**Dominant Arena**

House name

Short description

Number of significant activations.

Example:

2nd House · Value

Four major transits converge here today, making resources, security, material foundations, and self-worth the primary arena of development.

---

## Step 3 — Identify dominant planetary themes

Look for repeated activation of the same natal planets.

Example:

Moon + Saturn

Moon + Sun

Mercury + Saturn

Instead of interpreting them separately, determine what common developmental process they describe.

Generate one synthesis.

---

# Celestial Field

Display the strongest individual aspects.

Each aspect should contain only information unique to that aspect.

Do not repeat house explanations.

Do not repeat planet definitions.

Example:

Moon Conjunct Natal Saturn

Emotion illuminates where stronger structure is needed.

Mercury Square Natal Saturn

Mental friction exposes assumptions that require refinement.

Mars Sextile Natal Sun

Purpose receives practical momentum.

---

# Today's Theme

Generate one concise sentence (10–18 words).

It should summarize the entire field rather than any individual transit.

Examples:

Build what lasts.

Strengthen foundations before seeking expansion.

Consistency becomes more valuable than intensity.

Gather scattered effort into lasting form.

---

# Today's Energy

This section explains why the day feels the way it does.

Use synthesis.

Do not summarize every transit.

Instead answer:

Why does today feel this way?

What general rhythm emerges from the entire chart?

Mention only the dominant planets or houses responsible.

Target length:

120–180 words.

---

# What Is Being Refined

Generate one coherent alchemical metaphor.

This section represents the developmental process produced by the entire field.

Rules:

Only one metaphor.

Do not repeat the same symbolic idea using different language.

Avoid looping descriptions.

Build toward one concluding insight.

Target:

180–250 words.

---

# Forge Principle

Generate one memorable sentence.

Requirements:

Under 12 words.

Not motivational.

State a durable principle.

Examples:

Structure gives ambition somewhere to stand.

Attention becomes capacity through repetition.

Clarity grows where resistance is examined.

What is sustained becomes stronger.

---

# Journal Prompt

Generate exactly one reflective question.

It must arise naturally from the dominant house and overall synthesis.

Do not simply restate the house description.

---

# Daily Application

Generate three sections.

## Do

One concrete action.

Specific.

Observable.

Measurable.

## Avoid

One common behavior that disperses today's strongest potential.

## Practice

One repeatable exercise that develops today's dominant capacity.

Each should be under 50 words.

---

# Closing Reflection

End the report by translating today's celestial field into an enduring developmental principle.

Do not summarize the report.

Do not restate the transits.

Leave the reader with one idea they can continue practicing after today ends.

Target:

60–90 words.

---

# Writing Rules

Avoid repeating:

* House meanings
* Planet definitions
* Sign definitions
* The same developmental idea across multiple sections
* The same metaphor more than once

Every section must introduce new information.

Each section should answer a different question.

Theme:
What is today's essence?

Energy:
Why does today feel this way?

Refinement:
What inner capacity is being developed?

Principle:
What universal truth emerges?

Application:
How should this be lived?

Reflection:
What should remain after the day ends?

The report should read as one continuous progression rather than a collection of independently generated interpretations.
